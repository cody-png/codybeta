KIF Beta 0.21.62
- Auto-Battle: the game no longer freezes asking "Give up on learning?" when entry hazards knock out a foe as it comes in.
- Berserker keeps raising the other stats once Attack is maxed, without repeating "won't go any higher".
- Exp All sharing no longer gives part of your share to a partner trainer's Pokemon.
- Gym Leader extra Pokemon follow Single-Pokemon mode and Reversed mode like the rest of the team.
- The Starter K-Egg moves up a tier 10% of the time, as intended (it was 20%).
- PC Sort in Reverse Order keeps Pokemon with equal values in their old order.
- Big Icons show the new picture after you pick a new main sprite in the Pokedex.
- Mods: a mod that needs another mod that is Off or missing is shown as blocked instead of loading; a mod that replaces a game picture keeps the original and puts it back when the mod stops using it; a failed mod update is offered again.
- Updates: a game folder with % in its name no longer breaks the update window, and a failed update can't leave old files behind.
