KIF Beta 0.21.51
Options > Mods can now find, install and update mods:
- Browse lists the mods in the KIF-Mods catalog. Pick one to install it, together with any mods it needs.
- Updates lists your mods that have a newer version (Update all does them in one go). Their settings are kept.
- Installed shows ERROR for a mod that failed to load, and ON/OFF NEXT START after you switch one.
Search: press D (or Q) and type straight into the search bar; the list follows as you type. The key hints show the keys you have bound.
Every setting has a new, clearer description.
The Base Stats sliders only show while Base Stats is on.
Faster: the game's text is ready a few seconds sooner after start-up, and shuffling trainers in the Randomizer takes a few seconds instead of about 20.
The old settings pages are gone: Settings by category, mod settings and presets all open inside the settings screen.
