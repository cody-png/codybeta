KIF Beta 0.21.47
Battle Info has a new look with sharp, easy-to-read text, and new pages. Left/Right picks a Pokemon (or Field / Speed), Up/Down switches its pages:
- Stats: stat changes, ability, held item and effects, as before.
- Abilities: its ability with a description, plus extra abilities from mods (the Innates mod's innates). A foe's stay ??? until the battle shows them.
- Moves: type, category, power, accuracy and PP. A foe's moves show once it has used them, with how often.
- Speed: who moves first this turn (Trick Room, Tailwind, paralysis counted). Foes' Speed stays hidden.
Battle Info is now a standard feature: it no longer belongs to the Information module, so turning that module Off keeps it.
