KIF Beta 0.21.66
- Dominant Fusion Types work with the Randomizer's types: a species' dominant type is whatever type it has now in that place (Bulbasaur randomized to Ice makes Ice fusions).
- Shiny Trainer Pokemon: "Disabled" is now called "Normal": trainer Pokemon use the same shiny odds as your Wild Shiny Odds slider.
- Berserker: a foe that just switched in waits its turns before its first boost.
- "Skip this version" and the Updates setting stick even if you quit without saving.
- The Endgame Challenge ends when Battles & Challenges is switched Off (talking to the Fighting Arena leaders gave an error).
