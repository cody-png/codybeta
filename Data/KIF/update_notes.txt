KIF Beta 0.21.64
- Importing a Pokemon whose species the game doesn't have no longer turns it into a Pikachu; the file is kept for later.
- An imported Pokemon with an empty number field no longer crashes the PC.
- Loading an old KIF save no longer moves Pokemon in or out of the Hoenn Transfer Box, and Pokemon set aside are never lost if the conversion stops halfway.
- Endgame Challenge: beating the champion again after Mt. Silver and 12 Gym rematches unlocks Dem's challenge again; hard-mode Blue battles you whichever starter you picked; foes no longer gain extra PP each time they switch back in.
- Mods that copy one of KIF's item handlers no longer fail to load.
- The settings search and the trade bubbles use less work and memory.
