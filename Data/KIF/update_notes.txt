KIF Beta 0.21.54
- F12 (soft reset) now restarts the game properly. Before, F12 during a battle could break the foe's AI (errors every turn, the foe couldn't attack) and later saves could miss changes to your team.
- Animated sprites (sprites drawn as a strip of frames) no longer show every frame side by side; you see the first frame.
- Battle Info: the Speed page shows the foe's Speed too, so you can plan around it.
- Battle AI: Last Resort is no longer chosen while it can't work (a Pokémon that knew only Metronome and Last Resort used Last Resort every turn and failed).
