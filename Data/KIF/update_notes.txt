KIF Beta 0.21.34
EBDX battles are now opt-in: new players start with them off (Options > Modules > Graphical Changes settings > EBDX Battles). If you already have them on, nothing changes.
First start: "Classic KIF" means KIF as it was; newer features (EBDX battles, Move Effectiveness, Fusion Screen) start off.
Fusion screen: a fusion you haven't seen yet is a plain silhouette again (like PIF) when Fusion Preview is Off.
