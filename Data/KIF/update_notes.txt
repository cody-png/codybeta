KIF Beta 0.21.46
Multi-Battle Radar (Mixed): the other Pokemon in a radar 2v2/3v3 now roll their shininess with the chain's chance too, like Same Species.
Trade bubbles: every NPC who offers a trade now shows the trade bubble (PIF only had it on some, e.g. not the Cerulean trade).
PC: switching between full boxes is faster. A fusion's box icon is made much faster the first time it's shown, and a new box no longer draws its icons twice.
Shiny fusion icons: the party and the PC no longer mix up which half is shiny.
