KIF Beta 0.21.48
KIF Settings is rebuilt: one screen with sections on the left (Shinies, Battles, Pokemon, Graphics, Self-Battle, Challenges, Game, System, Modules, Mods, Presets) and sharp, easy-to-read text.
- Every setting shows its choices as a bar; Left/Right changes it. Names are shorter under their heading (Auto-Battle > Stop on Shiny).
- An orange dot marks a setting you changed; Z sets it back to its default. The bottom bar explains the setting and shows its default.
- A (or F / Tab) searches every setting by name and description.
- Modules, Mods and Presets are sections of the same screen. Options has one KIF Settings button.
