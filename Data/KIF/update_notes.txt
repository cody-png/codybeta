KIF Beta 0.21.52
Mods can now change KIF without replacing it:
- A mod can take over one KIF feature (for example Level Caps). Only that feature steps aside; the rest of KIF keeps working.
- A mod can set some settings or modules while it's On. Those rows show LOCKED and say which mod set them. Switch the mod Off and your own settings come back - they were kept the whole time.
- A mod made for another KIF version, or one that doesn't work with a mod you have On, isn't started. Options > Mods shows it as BLOCKED with the reason.
- Browse warns before you install a mod that won't start (WON'T START) or clashes with one you have (CLASH).
- Browse can list more than one catalog: other modders' catalogs show under KIF-Mods.
Key hints on the other screens (Pokédex, Fusion screen, Randomizer, Battle Info, updates) show the keys you have bound instead of letters like "A:" or "Z/Shift".
