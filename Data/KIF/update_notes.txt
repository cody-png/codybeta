KIF Beta 0.21.53
A big round of fixes from a full code check:
- Banning every legendary in the Randomizer no longer freezes the game: legendaries then become any allowed Pokémon.
- The Cell Battery and Magnet Stone (story items) are no longer shuffled by the item randomizer.
- The rival's "your starter" slot is kept again in randomized trainer teams.
- Updating a mod from Browse updates the copy you have, even if you renamed its folder. Options > Mods no longer contacts GitHub over and over after a failed Browse.
- The updater: a cut-short file list or a list with odd paths is refused; files you added are never removed; an update that stopped halfway is tried again on the next start.
- Sprite packs: a pack whose sprites couldn't all be written is kept (it was deleted), and packs that use an unsupported zip packing are handed to the backup unpacker.
- Import/Export: fusions, KIF types and number fields come back correctly; an imported file is renamed to .json.imported instead of deleted.
- Settings presets are written whole (a crash can't leave a broken file); a damaged one is kept as .bad.
- Save Backups (PIF): backups you named are never removed.
- Auto-Battle: your Pokémon keep badge boosts, Pickup and the like while the AI fights.
- Recover Consumables no longer gives you a partner trainer's items, or items from a Self-Battle copy.
- Trainer rematches: an evolved Pokémon no longer keeps an ability its new form can't have.
- Shiny fused Pokémon from K-Eggs show their shiny colours.
- The Randomizer's "skip prompts" window starts after the new data is made (it could run out on slow PCs).
- Powerful AI: a crash with Sticky Web and a damage estimate for some moves are fixed.
- Smaller: the frame-rate probes only run in debug mode; fewer leftover bitmaps after the Pokédex and fusion icons.
