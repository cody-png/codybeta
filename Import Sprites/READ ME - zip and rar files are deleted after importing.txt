Drop sprites here: loose .png files, a sprite pack folder, or the pack's .zip / .rar - any layout.
The next time the game starts, every sprite is moved to where the game reads it.

ZIP AND RAR FILES ARE DELETED once their sprites are installed, so keep your own copy
somewhere else if you want one. Or turn on Options > Others > Imported Archives: Keep,
and the archive is moved to the "Imported archives" folder next to Game.exe instead.

Sprites you already have are not overwritten without asking.
