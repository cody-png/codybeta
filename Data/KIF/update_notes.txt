KIF Beta 0.21.28
KIF now updates itself: at start-up it tells you when a new version is out and downloads only the files that changed. Your saves, mods and sprites are left alone.
Options > Modules > General settings: Updates (Stable / Beta / Off) and Check for Updates.
Elite Battle DX battle visuals are included (Graphical Changes settings).
The PC and party no longer pause on some Pokémon (new Sprite File Cache option).
No more Pokémon Infinite Fusion 2 messages or PIF installer prompts at start-up.
The fusion screen's green stats only mark real base-stat differences.
