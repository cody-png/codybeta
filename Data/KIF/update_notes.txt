KIF Beta 0.21.37
New: Battle Info. In battle, press Z / Shift while choosing Fight/Bag/Pokémon/Run to see stat changes, effects, weather and hazards (Information module).
Mods: a mod can now be a .rb file, a folder or a .zip in the Mods folder. Options > KIF Settings > Mods switches each one On/Off and opens its settings.
Fusion screen: unseen custom sprites are green (imported ones were blue) and autogens grey (white was invisible on the white box).
Losing to Misty no longer keeps her gym music playing in the Pokémon Center.
Linux / Steam Deck: after an update the game restarts by itself.
