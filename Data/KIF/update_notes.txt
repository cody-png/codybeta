KIF Beta 0.21.49
Options is now the new settings screen: PIF's own options (volume, text speed, screen size, difficulty, speed-up, sprites, challenge rules) sit in the same sections as KIF's.
- A section whose settings all belong to modules you turned Off is greyed out and skipped.
- Each module in Modules says which sections hold its settings.
- Buttons other mods add to Options are listed under Mods.
