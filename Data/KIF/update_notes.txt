KIF Beta 0.21.67
- Save Backups is always on: switching PC & Saves Off no longer lets the game delete old backups (named ones included).
- Saves the game makes by itself (Transfer Box, PC, Battle Frontier) keep their newest 10 backups, so the backups folder stops growing. Saves you make keep every backup, as before.
- Comatose works for every Pokemon that has it (random abilities, mods), not only Komala.
- Entrances: Return to Pokemon Center no longer sends you into a Center you can't leave, after Entrances were switched Off and On on a random-start game.
- Entrances: on a random-start game, Randomize now places trainer levels from your start, not from the badges you have now.
- Mods: when a mod stops replacing a game picture, the picture from your current KIF comes back (not an older one).
- Battle AI: its switch-in and entry-hazard choices can count abilities a mod adds (KIF: Elite Redux innates).
