KIF Beta 0.21.36
Options now has two buttons: Modules (turn features on/off) and KIF Settings (their settings, General settings, presets).
Poké Radar chains keep counting past 40 and keep improving the shiny odds past 20. A message tells you at 20 and 40 when the chain count is hidden.
New "Egg Moves" setting (Off by default): Change moves only offers egg moves when it's On.
Battle boxes: the status icon (PAR, PSN...) no longer hides behind the HP bar or shows up before the box does.
